reports `completed` and output files exist. Batch MaxRSS is Slurm's

These five job scripts each hashed the image on an allocated compute node;
they did not hash it on the login node. Do not reuse the per-case hashing
pattern: future campaigns should record one compute-job image digest and
reuse its immutable manifest across cases, per the Agent-guide rule.

they are not manuscript-ready. The separate two-L40S pilot job 1570379
